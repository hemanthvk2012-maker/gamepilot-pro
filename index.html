A STUDENT INNOVATOR STORY

Built by a Class 9 Maker for a Deaf Teammate.

During competitive ranked games in Valorant, my friend struggled to rank up not because of bad aim or mechanical skill, but because of severe hearing loss. While opponents could hear footsteps across walls, they fought in total audio silence.

I built GamePilot Pro from scratch using native C# and Win32 hardware windowing to prove that accessibility technology can match esports-tier speed without giving an unfair aim assist or risking game bans.

"When you remove physical barriers with clean code, skill is the only thing that remains."
