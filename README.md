## 💡 The Inspiration Story

I am a **Class 9 student**. I don't have a funded startup or a Silicon Valley team. I build between school, homework, and exams.

I have a close friend who is passionate about competitive gaming. He plays Valorant regularly, has solid mechanics, but kept getting stuck in ranked. Over and over, he'd lose rounds without understanding why—dying to silent flanks, missing spike defuse timings, and getting frustrated because the game itself gives zero meaningful feedback when you die.

Every gaming companion app on the market was:
1. Built for Western markets with expensive $15+/month subscriptions.
2. Focused on toxic stat roasts rather than real in-game tactical & accessibility assistance.
3. Completely ignoring players with audio challenges or color perception barriers.

I realized: **Why can't competitive gaming have a true tactical co-pilot that is lightweight, accessible, and affordable for student gamers in India?**

Having participated in national tech competitions and hackathons before, I decided to build **GamePilot Pro**. I used modern AI development tools—specifically Google's Antigravity AI pair programmer—to architect and write clean native code alongside my own design requirements.

> *"I used AI to code what I had not learned yet, but the vision, problem, and design came from real frustration on the ground. That is how our generation builds."*

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🛡️ 100% Riot Vanguard & Valve VAC Safe
- **Zero Memory Injection**: Never touches game process memory, DLLs, or VRAM.
- Operates strictly via non-invasive Win32 transparent overlays and official local client lockfile endpoints.

### 2. 🦻 Tactical & Deaf Accessibility Suite
- **Visual Audio Sonar**: Converts directional footsteps and gunfire into a subtle visual HUD sweep for hearing-impaired gamers.
- **45-Second Spike Timer**: Dynamic color-coded dial with half-defuse (3.5s) and full-defuse (7.0s) safe threshold warnings.
- **Recoil Cadence Metronome**: Visual pulse for burst reset timing to master spray control.
- **Colorblind Reticle Presets**: Deuteranopia, Protanopia, and Tritanopia contrast modes.

### 3. 🎬 ClipForge Vertical Reel Studio
- One-click capture (<kbd>F6</kbd>) into **9:16 vertical video format (1080x1920)**.
- Automatically generates upload manifests with viral hashtags for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok.

### 4. 📊 ACID JSON Match Analytics
- Stores complete match telemetry locally.
- Tracks KDA, Headshot %, Win Rate %, and clutch statistics.
- One-click CSV export to Desktop for review and coaching.
